Functionality:

1. Input source listing at Workstation
2. Maintain UOM conversion factors at "Item" master
3. Flow of Machine Specification Setting from Routing to BOM, BOM to Job Card
4. Items in the BOM will be mentioned in Secondary UOM . When fetched at Stock Entry, will be shown in Stock UOM
5. Allow user input to set base available qty at each Serial No on creation of GRN (Purchase Receipt) . Provided a floating window.
6. Serial No's fetch logic for "Material Transfer for Manufacture" - FIFO and available qty (ascending order). Also considering multiple spool assignment
7. Transfer and reserve the quantity on Stock Entry Submit of type "Material Transfer for Manufacture" if stock is unavailable at the Input Sources/Target Warehouse and Serial No's are required to be transferred from Source to Target Warehouse .
8. If stock already present at the Serial No's of Input Sources, reserve the quantity at the click of "Start" button at the Work Order
9. Tracking Serial No's available, reserved and consumed quantity Work Order wise at the "Serial No" doctype
10. Serial No's fetch logic for "Manufacture" - FIFO and based on reserved quantity at the Serial No
